# KITTY: A FARNDALE GHOST STORY

## Interactive Story Walk App – Prototype Project Brief

**Audio samples and three-station structure**

**Project:** 3-Minute Arts CIC – Interactive Story Walk
**Prototype:** Three location-based audio stations

---

## 1. Project overview

*Kitty: A Farndale Ghost Story* is a short dramatic audio story designed to be experienced as part of a location-based Story Walk in Farndale, North Yorkshire.

For the purposes of the Interactive Story Walk app prototype, the story has been divided into three sections, each associated with a separate stopping point (station) along the walk route. As walkers reach each location, the app will trigger the corresponding audio, allowing the story to unfold as they move through the landscape. Alternatively, users can choose to play the audio for a particular station manually at any point during the walk.

The three audio files supplied are to be used to create a prototype for the Interactive Story Walk app. The sample narration is to be used for testing the basic functionality of the app, including audio playback and the association of individual recordings with GPS locations.

## 2. Audio files and story structure

The three Kitty MP3 files (128 kbps) contain sample narration, mixed with ambient sound effects.

Each recording is a distinct episode in the narrative, with its own dramatic character and contribution to the unfolding story.

| Station | Dramatic quality | What the listener discovers |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Reverend and the River | Mystery and suspense | The appearance of Kitty's ghost, and Philip's first encounter with the river. |
| 2. The Secret of Sarkless Kitty | Dialogue, intrigue and character | The mother's strange attitude towards Kitty, and the shame and secrecy surrounding her story. |
| 3. The Restless Spirit | Atmosphere, tension and emotion | Philip's attempt to bring Kitty peace. |

## 3. Initial prototype objectives

The three audio samples are a starting point for developing and testing the app. The initial prototype should demonstrate:

- Three separate GPS locations, each associated with the correct audio station.
- The ability to trigger the appropriate recording when a walker reaches a designated location, together with a manual option to play the recordings. The manual playback option is important. GPS accuracy can vary, particularly in rural areas and wooded locations. Being able to select a station manually will make the experience more reliable and accessible.
- Reliable playback of the narration at each station.
- The ability to handle ambient sound independently of the spoken recordings.
- A simple, accessible interface that makes it clear to walkers when a new section of the story is available.

The prototype will use three sample locations and audio recordings to demonstrate the principle. Further development will explore how the app handles movement between stations, playback interruptions, GPS accuracy and the overall experience for walkers.

## 4. Future development

This is an initial work-in-progress prototype for 3-Minute Arts CIC. The intention is to explore how digital storytelling can bring local stories, folklore and landscape together in an engaging, place-based experience.

Once the basic functionality has been demonstrated, the prototype can be developed further using additional stories, location-specific recordings, environmental sound and a more detailed user interface.

Overall, the aim is to create an accessible Story Walk experience that encourages people to explore the landscape while discovering the stories connected with it.
